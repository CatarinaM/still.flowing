About Beth
BF
Beth Faria

Beth Faria has practised and taught in the Theravada tradition for over two decades, studying with teachers across Southeast Asia, Australia, and Brazil. Her teaching weaves together the precision of the Pali canon with a warm, embodied presence — making the path accessible to everyone who arrives.

She offers retreats and courses in Sydney and online for practitioners in Brazil, Australia, and beyond. All teachings are offered in English and Portuguese.

Beth também ensina em português — para praticantes no Brasil e em Portugal.
